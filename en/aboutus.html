First Steps

In 1992, Vinkom brought Kirby to Macedonia, beginning with the G3—a system that set a new standard for home hygiene. At a time when advanced deep-cleaning systems were almost unknown, the G3 drew attention through in-home demonstrations, where families could experience its power and efficiency first-hand. These demonstrations became a Vinkom hallmark, because the true value of the product is best shown in use. As a factory-authorized Kirby distributor with its own service centre and complete support for genuine parts and consumables, Vinkom built lasting trust with its customers. Its long-term commitment to one product is strong proof of the quality the system delivers. With more than three decades on the Macedonian market, the company continues to show that investing in Kirby pays off over time.

Growing Stronger

A year after the G3’s success, the G4 arrived in Macedonia—an improved model with a modern design and practical mechanical upgrades. Its power assistance made use noticeably easier, while its construction delivered reliable performance and long service life. Detailed in-home demonstrations introduced customers to the system’s capabilities, increasing confidence in the brand and strengthening Vinkom’s local position. The G4 brought together convenience, efficiency, and quality, setting the standard for later generations of Kirby systems.

In-home demonstrations gave customers a first-hand look at the G5’s innovation and quality, strengthening trust and loyalty in the brand.
