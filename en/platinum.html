3,200 RPM

At 3,200 revolutions per minute, the brush creates continuous and controlled mechanical agitation. A speed precisely balanced for optimal power and stability. The rotation remains consistent with every pass. This is where true deep-cleaning performance begins.

Controlled Power

The system maintains consistent power without fluctuations in speed. The mechanism delivers thorough cleaning without excessive force on surfaces. This stability is the result of precision engineering.

Built for Intensive Use

Continuous agitation is effective on pet hair and deeper buildup. Mechanical movement reaches where ordinary suction alone is not enough. The system is designed for high-traffic areas. Performance remains powerful and reliable over time.

Active Agitation

Rotational movement separates carpet and fabric fibers. Agitation reaches deeper than standard suction alone. The result is more effective and thorough cleaning.

Particle Release

Rotation loosens embedded dust and fine particles trapped between the fibers. The system actively releases buildup rather than simply passing over it. Once loosened, particles can be more easily extracted from the material. The difference is noticeable from the very first use.

FAQ

FAQ

Can I pick up water with my Kirby system?

No. Do not use your Kirby to get water off the floor or out of carpet.

How much carpet shampoo am I supposed to use in the shampoo system?

First and foremost, only use genuine Kirby carpet shampoo with your system. Unscrew the large-cap from the shampoo tank and use it to measure carpet shampoo. Do not use the cap from the bottle of the shampoo to measure. For a small room, fill the tank with water to the first line on the side of the tank and add one capful of shampoo. To clean a large room, fill the tank to the third line and add three capfuls of shampoo.

How come there is no foam coming out of the shampoo system?

To produce foam properly, the suds screen and foam sponge must be in place on the shampoo tank. To check this, remove the tank and make sure they’re properly in place.

Why is the carpet so wet after shampooing?

This could be for one of two reasons. The suds screen and/or foam sponge are not in place. If not in place, this would allow too much water to dispense, over-wetting your carpet. Repeatedly cleaning the same area would also cause this. To avoid over-wetting moving forward, allow the system to dispense a layer of foam on the carpet and then turn the valve on the shampoo tank to OFF before going back over the area to work in the foam and scrub the carpet.
